About Us

ArkRock STEM PreSchool is a warm, nurturing environment dedicated to fostering a strong foundation in science, technology, engineering, and mathematics for young children.

We combine academic rigor customized for every student with social-emotional learning and executive functioning skills. Our predominantly engineer-led staff brings real passion for teaching and discovery.

Parents receive daily photo updates so you stay connected to your child’s joyful learning journey.

Quick Facts

  • Ages: 2½ (36 months) – 6 years
  • Hours: Mon–Fri 9:00 AM – 1:00 PM
  • Location: Bellevue, WA
  • Focus: Hands-on STEM + curiosity

Our Mission

Children love fun hands-on projects. They become curious when they see a rocket fly high or a caterpillar transform into a beautiful butterfly.

Our goal is to make them think and ask “why?” — so they become lifelong explorers.

We are committed to providing affordable, high-quality STEM preschool education in a secure and nurturing setting that empowers every child.

STEM Curriculum

Our curriculum is designed to inculcate curiosity through real exploration. Topics grow with the seasons and children’s interests.

🌱

Life Science

Living & non-living things, life cycles, growing plants, butterflies, nutrition

🧲

Physical Science

Magnetism, wind, forces, simple machines, making dyes & color exploration

🚀

Engineering & Design

Building rockets, structures, problem-solving challenges, creative construction

🔢

Math Foundations

Counting, patterns, measurement, spatial reasoning woven into every project
